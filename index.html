Free Strip Shows & Sights

Zero ticket cost • Verified schedule

Bellagio Fountains & Conservatory

Daily Free

Fountains play every 30 mins afternoon; every 15 mins after 8 PM until midnight. Indoor Botanical Conservatory is open 24/7.

Fall of Atlantis (Caesars Palace)

Hourly

Inside Forum Shops by the saltwater aquarium. Moving 9-foot animatronic statues, fireballs, and water effects enacting Atlantis's downfall.

Free Circus Acts (Circus Circus)

Midday Daily

Aerialists, high-flying trapeze artists, and foot-jugglers on the Carnival Midway stage.

Lake of Dreams (Wynn)

Every 30 Mins

Runs from dusk until 11:30 PM. Holographic laser shows, animatronic puppetry, and music over the 40-foot waterfall.

Viva Vision Light Canopy (Fremont)

Hourly 6PM–2AM

Massive 5-block digital LED canopy 90 feet overhead playing synchronized concerts and visuals.
