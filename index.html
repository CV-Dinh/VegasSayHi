🍜 Food & Drink Bucket List

🥢 Must-Eat Spots

  • Famous Foods Street Eats: Located at Resorts World. Hawker-style Southeast Asian market (claypot rice, skewers, Hainanese chicken).
  • Tacos El Gordo: Authentic Tijuana adobada carved fresh off the trompo (North Strip location right across from Resorts World).
  • Phở Đà Lạt: Authentic Vietnamese beef phở & com tam on W Flamingo Rd (Chinatown corridor).
  • Molly Tea (茉莉奶白): Popular fresh floral/jasmine milk-tea foam boba on S Jones Blvd.

✨ Free Shows & Sights

  • Bellagio Fountains & Conservatory: Spectacular dancing water show + botanical holiday exhibit.
  • Fall of Atlantis: Animatronic fire & water mythological show at The Forum Shops (Caesars Palace).
  • Circus Circus Midway: Free daily world-class acrobatics and trapeze performances.
  • Wynn Lake of Dreams: Holographic visuals, animatronics, and 40-ft waterfall spectacle.
  • Fremont Street Viva Vision: 5-block overhead LED light canopy shows.
  • Flamingo Wildlife Habitat: Real Chilean flamingos, pelicans, and waterfalls.

💡 Pro Tips For Your Trip
  • Toledo to DTW Drive: Allow at least 50–55 minutes via I-75 N to I-275 N at that early hour, plus parking shuttle time.
  • Early Arrival Advantage: Arriving at 8:39 AM means you gain over 3 extra hours in Vegas on Friday morning! Drop bags at the Luxor bell desk immediately and enjoy brunch or sightseeing.
  • MGM Digital Check-in ID Rule: First-time users still need to swing by the quick Mobile Check-In desk with a physical government ID + payment card to verify before digital keys unlock.
  • Tacos El Gordo Lines: Each meat has its own line. Go straight to the Adobada line, order your tacos, take the plate, and pay at the register.
  • SlotZilla Booking: Thanksgiving weekend gets busy; reserve your SlotZilla nighttime slot online in advance.
